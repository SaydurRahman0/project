Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers regarding our booking policies, products, and what to expect during your first visit.

While we warmly welcome walk-ins when styling stations are open, we strongly recommend scheduling your appointment in advance to secure your preferred artist and time slot, especially on Fridays, Saturdays, and holidays.
We understand schedules change! We kindly request at least 24 hours notice for any cancellation or reschedule so we may offer that dedicated time to guests on our waiting list. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice may incur a 30% service fee.
We exclusively partner with certified clean, cruelty-free, and sustainable salon brands including Davines, Oribe, Dermalogica Professional, and K-Gloss Organic. All our color systems are ammonia-free or low-ammonia.
We recommend booking your bridal trial 2 to 4 months prior to your wedding date. This allows plenty of time to customize your bridal look, coordinate headpieces or veils, and schedule any pre-wedding skin prep treatments.
Your safety and peace of mind are paramount. All manicure and pedicure metal tools are autoclaved using hospital-grade dry heat sterilization, nail files and buffers are single-use, and all treatment suites are thoroughly sanitized between guests.
